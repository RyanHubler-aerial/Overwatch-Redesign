How to Replace the Console Battery on a 2GIG GC3 Panel

Required Battery: Li-Polymer 823990 / 10-000014-001 (3800mAh / 3.7V). Replacement batteries can be purchased directly from OverWatch Security by calling (877) 359-4407 or emailing support@overwatchus.com.

View Step-by-Step Replacement & Alert-Clearing Instructions

Replacing the Battery

  1. Disarm the system before opening the control panel.
  2. Loosen the bottom screw located at the bottom center of the control panel housing.
  3. Use the interior plastic strap to hook and hang the panel safely from the mounted backplate while you work.
  4. Disconnect and remove the old battery by unplugging its terminal connector and lifting it out of the battery bay.
  5. Insert the new battery into the slot inside the control panel and reconnect the new battery cable to the console terminal.
  6. Re-mount the panel securely onto the backplate and tighten the bottom retaining screw.

Clearing Panel Fault or Trouble Alerts

After replacing the battery, you may see existing fault or trouble messages on the touchscreen. To acknowledge and clear them:

  1. Press the message icon on the top bar of the 2GIG GC3 screen.
  2. Press Alerts to view active trouble notices.
  3. Review the alert, then select Acknowledge or Clear to remove it.

If the trouble fault returns after replacement, please contact OverWatch Security support at (877) 359-4407 or support@overwatchus.com.

How to Pay Your OverWatch Invoice via Email

Settle your OverWatch Security invoice directly from your email using our secure SecurityTrax payment portal—supporting both ACH electronic bank transfers and credit card payments.

View Step-by-Step Payment Instructions
  1. Locate the invoice email: Look in your inbox for an email with a subject line reading “Your Invoice from OverWatch Security” (check your spam or junk folder if you do not see it).
  2. Review the invoice attachment: Open the email and check the attached invoice to confirm your amount and due date.
  3. Click “Pay Now”: Select the Pay Now button inside the email to open the secure SecurityTrax payment page.
  4. Choose your payment method:
    • ACH (Electronic Bank Transfer): Select ACH, enter your bank routing and account numbers, and confirm your details.
    • Credit Card: Select Credit Card, enter your card number, expiration date, and CVV code, and verify your information.
  5. Submit payment: Click Submit Payment. You will receive an email confirmation as soon as the transaction processes.

Getting Started Fundamentals

How Security Systems Work: A Beginner’s Guide

New to alarm systems? Here is a clear breakdown of the six core components inside a modern residential or commercial security system and how they work together during an event.

Read the Complete Beginner’s Guide

Key Components of a Modern Security System

  • The Control Panel: The system’s central “brain” that communicates with every sensor, camera, and siren. Modern panels feature touchscreens, cellular communicators, and mobile app control.
  • Sensors: Devices that monitor entry points and interior spaces—including door and window contacts, motion detectors, glass-break sensors, and discreet panic buttons for immediate emergency signaling.
  • Security Cameras: Indoor and outdoor HD cameras providing live feeds, infrared night vision, motion-triggered recording, and mobile clip notifications.
  • Alarms & Sirens: High-decibel audible alerts designed to deter intruders immediately and alert occupants or nearby neighbors.
  • 24/7 Monitoring Service: Professional central-station operators watching your system around the clock to verify alerts and dispatch police, fire, or medical responders when needed.
  • Backup Power: Built-in battery backup keeps your panel and dedicated cellular link online if primary power fails.

How Detection, Alerting & Response Work

  1. Detection: A sensor or camera detects unauthorized opening, motion, glass break, smoke, or carbon monoxide.
  2. Alert: The control panel triggers the local siren, pushes an instant alert to your smartphone, and transmits the event over dedicated cellular to the monitoring center.
  3. Response: Monitoring specialists verify the signal and immediately dispatch local emergency responders if required.

Choosing the Right Architecture

Modern systems can be hardwired, wireless (cellular/encrypted RF), or hybrid—tailored around your property size, fire-code requirements, camera coverage goals, and door access needs. Ready for a custom recommendation? Schedule a walkthrough with our team.
