Too many national security companies ship one-size-fits-all hardware, lock customers into rigid call centers, and disappear once the box is mounted on the wall.

OverWatch Security was built around the opposite model. Operating out of Bedford, PA as a division of Aerial Communications (Kevin A. Diehl LLC), we bring disciplined low-voltage engineering and hands-on field service to homes, medical offices, municipal facilities, and commercial sites across Southcentral Pennsylvania.

Because our roots are in commercial communications and critical infrastructure, we sweat the details that matter when something goes wrong: dedicated cellular alarm paths that keep reporting even if phone or internet lines fail, clean wire routing, properly aimed cameras, and clear documentation for every zone in your building.

Whether you are protecting a family home or managing life safety across a commercial campus, you work directly with the same local team from walkthrough through installation, annual inspections, and ongoing support.
